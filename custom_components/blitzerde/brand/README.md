# Brand assets

These images are original neutral artwork for the Blitzer.de Home Assistant
community integration.

They intentionally do **not** reproduce the Blitzer.de corporate logo and do
not use Home Assistant branding. Their purpose is to identify this custom
integration without implying endorsement.

Files:

- `icon.png` — 256 × 256 PNG
- `icon@2x.png` — 512 × 512 PNG

The icon depicts a generic traffic-sign circle and camera lens. It may be
regenerated or replaced with other original artwork in a future release.
